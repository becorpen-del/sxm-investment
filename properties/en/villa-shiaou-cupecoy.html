Discover refined Caribbean living at Villa Shiao

Extraordinary lagoonfront estate in Cupecoy.

This 8-bedroom, 7-bathroom residence blends elegance, privacy, and flexibility — ideal as a prestigious family home, vacation retreat, or high-yield investment property.

Enjoy direct access to the lagoon, complete with water rights and a permit for a 20-meter private yacht dock — a rare opportunity for boat owners and yachting enthusiasts. Set within a secure, gated tropical enclave, the property is surrounded by lush landscaping and offers ample parking for more than eight vehicles.

Two independent one-bedroom guest apartments, each with private access and water-view terraces, provide excellent options for generating rental income, hosting guests, or housing staff.

The unfinished penthouse level offers a blank canvas for bespoke creation — whether as a guest suite, private office, wellness sanctuary, or entertainment lounge. Designed for comfort and resilience, the villa features a pool deck, backup generator, outdoor sound system, air conditioning throughout, and full hurricane protection.

The Neighborhood

Cupecoy is one of Sint Maarten’s most sought-after areas, celebrated for its combination of luxury living, natural beauty, and convenience. Nestled along the Caribbean Sea, it offers sweeping water views, calm beaches, and dramatic cliffside vistas.

The neighborhood is home to upscale resorts, fine dining, cafés, and boutiques, and is located just minutes from Princess Juliana International Airport. Residents enjoy a vibrant yet relaxed lifestyle with nearby golf, tennis, and a full range of water sports, along with easy access to the French side of the island. Cupecoy perfectly balances privacy, security, and exclusivity with everything needed for modern Caribbean living.
