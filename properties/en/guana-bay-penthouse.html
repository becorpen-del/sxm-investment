The Neighborhood

Guana Bay, located on the southeastern coast of St. Maarten, is a serene and secluded beach that offers an idyllic retreat just a short drive from the bustling capital, Philipsburg. This proximity to town means you can easily enjoy the vibrant shopping, dining, and entertainment options before retreating to the peaceful ambiance of Guana Bay. Additionally, it's conveniently close to the French side of the island, allowing for easy exploration of both Dutch and French cultures during your stay. With its rugged cliffs, pristine sands, and clear waters, Guana Bay is perfect for those seeking a tranquil escape without being far from the island's main attractions.
