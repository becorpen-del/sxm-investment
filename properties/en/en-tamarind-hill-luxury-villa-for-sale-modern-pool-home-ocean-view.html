Description

This property offers a rare opportunity to own a spectacular 9-bedroom, 9-bathroom villa in the exclusive gated community of Tamarind Hill Estates. Combining luxury, privacy, and strong investment potential, the villa features a large private pool on the main level and an expansive terrace with breathtaking views of St. Barth.

The upper level serves as the main residence, with 4 spacious bedrooms that open directly onto the pool area, creating a seamless indoor–outdoor living experience. The generous terrace is perfect for relaxing or entertaining, with sweeping ocean views that make every moment feel special.

On the ground level, the villa includes 4 separate income-generating apartments, each with its own private entrance: one 2-bedroom unit and three 1-bedroom units. This configuration allows you to maximize rental income while maintaining complete privacy in the main villa above.

Additional features include a large, fully equipped gym, a dedicated laundry facility for the apartments, and parking for up to 12 vehicles. The property is fully outfitted with split A/C, ceiling fans, an alarm system, a durable zinc roof, and a private saltwater pool.

Ideally located just 10 minutes from the French side, the villa offers easy access to the famous beaches of Orient Bay and the gourmet restaurants of Grand Case. It is an exceptional opportunity for those seeking both a luxury Caribbean lifestyle and a high-yield rental property.

The Neighborhood
Located on the eastern coast of Sint Maarten, Tamarind Hill is an exclusive residential area known for its absolute tranquility and breathtaking views of the Caribbean Sea and St. Barth. Surrounded by lush natural beauty and secured by gated access, it is the ideal setting for a peaceful escape.

Its proximity to the French side means that popular destinations such as Orient Bay and Grand Case are just a short drive away, offering pristine beaches, fine dining, and vibrant nightlife. Tamarind Hill lets you experience the island’s authentic charm in a luxurious and serene environment.
