Description

Recently updated with top-notch finishes, new designer furnishings, and a brand-new fitness center.


Perched elegantly on the lush hills of Pelican Key, Villa Oceane is a symbol of opulence in Dutch St. Maarten. Offering a stunning 180-degree view, this Mediterranean-style gem presents a sweeping sight of the vibrant Caribbean Sea along with the captivating islands of Saba, St. Eustatius, St. Kitts, and Nevis.


Seamlessly integrated into the lush surroundings, the villa showcases exceptional craftsmanship, featuring high-quality materials and flawless design throughout. A lush garden welcomes visitors at the main entrance, leading them into the impressive interior. Upon entering the main level, guests are greeted by a spacious living area embellished with floor-to-ceiling windows that capture the stunning scenery. Beyond this space lies a vast terrace that beautifully combines indoor and outdoor living, leading to a pristine pool deck.


The well-equipped kitchen, complete with granite countertops and a separate pantry, serves as a culinary paradise with access to an underground wine cellar for enthusiasts.


The master suite, a haven of sophistication, includes a generous walk-in closet and a luxurious en-suite bathroom featuring both a shower and a tub, ensuring a lavish retreat. Ascending to the upper level, two equally-sized bedrooms each come with private bathrooms, providing comfort and seclusion for all visitors.


Outside, an expansive deck awaits, featuring a shimmering pool and a delightful gazebo next to a fully-equipped BBQ area, perfect for outdoor dining and entertaining under the sun or stars. Descending to the lower level, a gym and sauna facing the ocean provide wellness and rejuvenation against a backdrop of sweeping views.


Additional features include a pump/storage area, laundry facilities, and a flexible multi-purpose room ideal for an office, guest accommodations, or a cozy home theater escape.


Adjacent to the main villa is a separate one-bedroom apartment, offering independent access and featuring a half-mezzanine bedroom overlooking the living area and kitchen. Complete with its own laundry facilities and a spacious terrace with ocean views, it provides comfort and convenience for guests seeking a private retreat.


Discover the pinnacle of Caribbean luxury living at Villa Oceane, where every detail is designed with exquisite taste and unparalleled quality.
