Description

Experience unparalleled waterfront living in this sophisticated penthouse with a rooftop at Residence Le Papillon, one of the most sought-after locations in Simpson Bay. Covering 315 sqm along with an additional 80 sqm private rooftop, the residence offers sweeping views of the bay, beach, garden, and the Caribbean Sea.

Inside, you will find 3 generously sized ensuite bedrooms complemented by a guest half-bath, all connected to a bright open-plan living space. Comfort features include central air conditioning, ceiling and portable fans, a designated workspace, and an in-unit washer/dryer. Secure remote-entry parking for two vehicles and private elevator access provide both convenience and privacy.

The outdoor lifestyle is enhanced with a fully enclosed backyard, a private hot tub, an outdoor shower, and a rooftop kitchen complete with a gas BBQ, dining area, and lounge seating, creating a resort-like atmosphere.

This penthouse seamlessly combines contemporary luxury with Caribbean allure, making it perfect as a distinguished residence or an outstanding investment opportunity.
