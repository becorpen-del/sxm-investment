Description

Welcome to your dream Caribbean lifestyle at our luxurious villa nestled in the prestigious neighborhood of Almond Grove, Sint Maarten.

This impressive residence boasts five bedrooms, thoughtfully distributed throughout the property to provide ample space and privacy. On the main floor, three bedrooms offer comfort and elegance, while a separate one-bedroom apartment and an independent studio provide additional accommodation options for guests or extended family members.

With five bathrooms, including en-suites for each bedroom, convenience and luxury are seamlessly woven into every corner of this home.

Indulge in the breathtaking views of St. Maarten from the comfort of your own sanctuary. The villa's scenic vistas provide a serene backdrop to everyday life, offering a constant reminder of the beauty that surrounds you. Inside, the design exudes refinement, with generously proportioned living spaces and a gourmet kitchen equipped with premium appliances and an expansive layout, perfect for entertaining guests or enjoying quiet family dinners.

Step outside to your private oasis, where a tranquil pool awaits amidst lush greenery, providing the ideal setting for relaxation and rejuvenation. An outdoor dining area with a charming barbecue completes the experience, offering the perfect spot for al fresco meals with loved ones.

Practicality meets luxury with amenities such as a walk-in dressing room, providing ample storage for wardrobe essentials, and secure parking including a private driveway and a spacious closed garage, ensuring peace of mind and convenience for residents and guests alike.
