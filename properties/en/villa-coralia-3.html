The Neighborhood

Orient Bay, located on the northeastern coast of Saint Martin, is one of the island’s most popular destinations, known for its stunning white-sand beach and turquoise waters. Often referred to as the “Saint-Tropez of the Caribbean,” it offers a lively atmosphere with a range of beach clubs, restaurants, and water sports. Visitors can enjoy activities like kite surfing, jet skiing, and parasailing, while the nearby coral reefs provide excellent snorkeling opportunities. The bay is surrounded by luxury villas, including Villa Coralia, making it an ideal spot for both relaxation and adventure amidst beautiful natural scenery.
