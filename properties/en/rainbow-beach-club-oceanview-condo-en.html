Description

Discover the true meaning of island living at Rainbow Beach Club, one of the most sought-after beachfront communities in Cupecoy. This meticulously maintained 2-bedroom, 2-bathroom condominium offers 112 m² (1,205 sq ft) of bright and welcoming living space, featuring stunning panoramic views of the Caribbean Sea and Cupecoy Bay.


Residents at Rainbow Beach Club benefit from a variety of resort-style amenities, including three swimming pools with ocean views, a tennis court, beautifully landscaped gardens, a gazebo lounge area, and direct beach access through a private staircase carved into the cliff. The community also provides underground parking, 24/7 gated security, a backup generator, and elevator access for maximum convenience and peace of mind.


Fully furnished and ready for immediate occupancy, this condominium is perfect as a primary residence, vacation getaway, or a high-yield investment property in one of St. Maarten’s most desirable neighborhoods.
