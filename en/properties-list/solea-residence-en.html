Solea Residence – Upscale Condos in Little Bay, St. Maarten

Discover Solea Residence, an exclusive collection of premium condos in the heart of Little Bay, just 300 meters from the beach.

Features: Exceptional panoramic views of Little Bay Beach. Large covered terraces with built-in dining areas. Luxury finishes: Italian ceramic tiles, quartz countertops, wooden cabinetry.

Safety: Reinforced concrete structure, secured entrance doors, aluminum sliding windows. One of the safest developments on the island. Air conditioning in all units.

Prime Location: 5-minute walk to Great Bay Beach and downtown Philipsburg. 20 minutes from the international airport, 5 minutes from the cruise terminal.

The Neighborhood: Little Bay offers tranquility, privacy, and spectacular views while remaining close to the capital's vibrant atmosphere.

Competitive pricing for spacious living areas and arguably the best ocean views in St. Maarten.
