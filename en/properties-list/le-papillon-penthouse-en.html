Description

Discover unparalleled beachside living in this elegant penthouse with rooftop terrace at Le Papillon Residence, one of the most exclusive addresses in Simpson Bay, St. Martin. Spanning 315 m² with an additional 80 m² private rooftop terrace, the home offers panoramic views of the bay, beach, garden, and the Caribbean Sea.

Inside, 3 spacious bedrooms with ensuite bathrooms and a guest half-bath complement an open and luminous living space. Comfort amenities include central air conditioning, ceiling and portable fans, a dedicated workspace, and in-unit washer/dryer. Secured parking with remote entry for two cars and private elevator access ensure convenience and privacy.

The outdoor lifestyle is elevated with a fully fenced garden, a private jacuzzi, an outdoor shower, and a rooftop kitchen equipped with a gas barbecue, dining area, and lounge seating, completing the resort-style experience.

This penthouse perfectly blends modern luxury with Caribbean charm, ideal as a prestigious residence or an exceptional investment property.
