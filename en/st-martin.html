Welcome to St. Martin

St. Martin is a paradise island located in the Caribbean, shared between two nations: France in the north (Saint-Martin) and the Netherlands in the south (Sint Maarten). This unique duality offers the best of both worlds: European culture, refined gastronomy, and an advantageous tax environment.

With its 37 white sand beaches, crystal clear turquoise waters and idyllic tropical climate, St. Martin has become one of the most sought-after Caribbean destinations for savvy real estate investors.

An Exceptional Living Environment

St. Martin offers an incomparable lifestyle. Imagine waking up every morning with a view of the Caribbean Sea, enjoying world-renowned gourmet restaurants, and exploring pristine beaches accessible within minutes.

The island enjoys a pleasant tropical climate year-round, with temperatures ranging between 25°C and 30°C (77°F-86°F). The cosmopolitan international community creates a welcoming and dynamic atmosphere.

A Dynamic Real Estate Market

The St. Martin real estate market has experienced sustained growth for several years. Demand for luxury properties remains strong, driven by an international clientele seeking second homes or rental investments.

Rental yields are particularly attractive, especially in the vacation rental sector which benefits from a high occupancy rate throughout the year.

Why Invest in St. Martin?

Tax Advantages

Benefit from an advantageous tax regime with exemptions on capital gains and reduced property taxes compared to mainland France.

Attractive Returns

Rental yields can reach 8-12% annually thanks to strong demand for vacation rentals and a wealthy international clientele.

Monetary Stability

On the French side, you invest in euros in a European Union territory, guaranteeing stability and legal security.

Accessibility

International airport with direct flights from Paris, Amsterdam, New York and many other major cities.

Quality of Life

37 beaches, over 300 restaurants, vibrant nightlife and exceptional water activities all year round.
